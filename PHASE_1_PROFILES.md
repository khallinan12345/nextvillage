## ## Profile 1: Young Vocational Learner (Entrepreneur/Tradesperson)

### *Goals:*
- Acquire practical skills in chosen trade (small business, commerce, crafts)
- Earn recognized credentials to improve income or employment prospects

### *Language Background:*
- Native speaker of Nigerian Pidgin; fluent in spoken communication
- Can read basic English text; **blocked by abstract, high-level business jargon** (e.g., "entrepreneurship," "stakeholder," "revenue")
- Prefers Pidgin explanations and word breakdowns; benefits from translations of technical terms

### *Platform Frustrations (NextVillage-specific):*
- Business/vocational terminology in English is too abstract; learners cannot navigate or understand lessons
- Certificate claim process unclear; supervisor approval requirement not explained in UI

### *Estimated Cohort Weight:* - ~50% of the 15-20 learner cohort (To be validated via hybrid roster audit)

## ## Profile 2: Seasoned Vocational Practitioner (Fisherman/Farmer)

### *Goals:*
- Learn new techniques or business practices to improve productivity or income
- Access knowledge without reliance on reading

### *Language Background:*
- Native speaker of Nigerian Pidgin; fluent in spoken communication
- **Very low English literacy; reading is a significant barrier to all platform access**
- Requires voice-driven interactions and audio guidance; text-heavy layouts are exclusionary

### *Platform Frustrations (NextVillage-specific):*
- Text-heavy UI and navigation labels in English completely block access
- No audio playback or voice input alternatives available
- Cannot use platform without an intermediary reader

### *Estimated Cohort Weight:* - ~30% of the 15-20 learner cohort (To be validated via hybrid roster audit)

---

## ## Profile 3: Literate Sector Learner (Healthcare/Advisory Track)

### *Goals:*
- Complete certification in healthcare or advisory sector
- Progress through content matched to their age and experience level

### *Language Background:*
- Native speaker of Nigerian Pidgin; moderate English literacy
- Can read English; prefers Pidgin or bilingual content for clarity
- No significant language barrier to content comprehension

### *Platform Frustrations (NextVillage-specific):*
- Content difficulty does not scale to match learner's age and experience
- Certificate claim button locked; supervisor approval requirement not explained in UI
- Unclear what action is required to unlock certification pathway

### *Estimated Cohort Weight:* - ~20% of the 15-20 learner cohort (To be validated via hybrid roster audit)
