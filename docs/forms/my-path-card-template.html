My path with nextVillage

Learning to think, build and lead
with AI · nextVillage.community
Why we are doing this. The world of work is changing fast, and AI is part of almost every job. Our goal is to prepare young people for the modern world: to build the thinking, communication and technical skills that employers and communities need, so they can earn, lead and create opportunity close to home.

What is nextVillage?

A learning platform where young people work with an AI tutor, guided by a trusted leader in their own community. They learn to ask good questions, check what AI tells them, build real things, and use what they learn to help people around them. It is not a game or a shortcut. It is practice for real work.

The path, one step at a time (tick each box when it is done)

0☐AI DiscoveryAbout five short sessions: give AI clear instructions and make a robot, a character, a story and a game.
1☐AI FoundationsWhat AI is, how to ask it well, when it is wrong, and how to use it responsibly. Certificate: AI Proficiency.
2☐AI-Ready SkillsThinking clearly, solving problems, creativity and communication, with AI as a partner. Certificate: AI Ready Skills.
3☐Vibe CodingBuild a working app by describing it, testing it and improving it. Certificate: Vibe Coding.
4☐Web DevelopmentUnderstand and write real website code, line by line. Certificate: Web Development.
5☐Full-Stack CapstoneBuild a complete application with a database and user accounts. Certificate: Full-Stack.

Each step opens when the one before is passed. Every learner moves at their own pace, and a certificate can be retaken until it is earned. Alongside the path: English, Math and Science practice, free exploring, and projects that help the community.

What your child gains

  • Clear communication and critical thinking
  • Problem solving and creativity
  • Digital skills and using AI responsibly
  • Real projects and certificates as proof of what they can do
  • Confidence to lead, teach others and help the community

How we keep your child safe

  • A parent or guardian signs a consent form for every learner under 18.
  • A trained leader is present, and a named child safety officer follows up on any worry.
  • Personal details are removed before text goes to the AI. We never sell information and never show advertisements.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your child's information at any time.

How you can help at home

  • Ask: “What did you make or learn today?” and ask them to show you.
  • Encourage them to check what AI says before believing it.
  • Remind them: never share a password, never type phone numbers or addresses to the AI, and tell a leader about anything that worries them.
  • Celebrate each step finished. Progress matters more than speed.
