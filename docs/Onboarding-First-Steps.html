From Sign-Up to First Steps

What a new site leader actually walks through — from creating an account to the moment their first learner opens AI Discovery and moves on to the Core Path.

Who this is for: a community leader, teacher, or organization considering nextVillage — a plain walk-through of the signup flow and the first steps after it, before you commit any time to it.

1

Create an account

An email address and password, or Google/GitHub sign-in. Nothing about your community is asked yet — this step is just proving you're a real person.

2

Choose your role

Teacher / Educator, then Register My Organization — this makes you the primary leader of a new site. (A second adult helping you run the lab instead chooses Join as Co-Facilitator with your join code — see step 6.)

4

Say how long we should keep learner data

optional

90 days, a year, the length of your program, until you ask us to delete it, or simply "not sure yet." Nothing here is locked in — it's a starting position for a conversation, not a binding setting.

5

Pick the tools your learners will use

optional — all on by default

Every tool is enabled unless you turn one off. We recommend leaving all five on for a first cohort, and revisiting the choice with your learners and co-facilitators once you've seen how each one is actually used.

Foundations — English / Math / Science AI Proficiency Tech Skills Creative AI Community Impact AI
6

Receive your join code

The platform generates a unique 6-character code the moment your organization is created. Every learner and co-facilitator enters it at signup to link to your site, your location, and your community's localized lessons.

DAV001

A sample code, shown the size and shape yours will be. Yours appears once, at signup, and afterward lives on your Profile page — where you can also generate more.

7

Bring your learners in

Share the code before anyone signs up. Each learner then chooses Student / Learner and enters it.

  • Ideally, each learner signs up with their own email address — it keeps their account, progress, and any password reset theirs alone.
  • A learner without an email of their own can still sign up; talk through what that means for account recovery before they start.
  • You can always find your code — and generate new ones for a new cohort — on your Profile page.
8

Start with AI Discovery

Whatever else your site plans to use, every new learner's very first activity should be AI Discovery — a short, guided on-ramp before Foundations, Tech Skills, or any of the community-impact tools.

Recommended first activity

It's the one activity every learner does in common, whatever tools your site chooses later. Open the AI Discovery Guide →

9

Then, the Core Path

After AI Discovery, every learner moves into Core Curriculum → Core Path — the one ordered curriculum every learner follows next, not a free-choice catalog. It pairs AI-facilitated learning and training with real certification exams, in order: AI Proficiency, then AI Ready Skills, then Tech Skills. Each step stays locked until the certification for the step before it is actually passed.

Where learners go next

This is the default track for a new community's students — no separate setup needed on your part. Open the Core Path Guide →
