# Leader guide: protecting learners' information (one page)

For group leaders and co-leaders (for example in Oloibiri and Dayton). Keep it short; ask Kevin if unsure.

**Child protection lead: Confidence Onuoha** (WhatsApp +234 806 417 7653, confidence.onuoha@nextVillage.community). Parents and leaders can go to her with any child-safety worry or in-person privacy request; she tells Kevin.

## Your five jobs

1. **Approve each new member yourself, and only if you know them.** On your dashboard, "Join requests".
   For anyone **under 18**, confirm that a parent or guardian agreed (signed form, or in person) and say how.
   You can approve a group with one confirmation if you hold all of their forms.
2. **Record the consent forms you already hold, but only the right kind.** A form must cover **using the platform and sending information abroad**. The old media and publication form alone does not; those families also sign the one-page add-on first. On your dashboard, "Learners under 18 without a recorded
   consent": select the learners whose signed forms you have, choose "signed form", tick the confirmation.
   **Keep the paper forms safe** (a locked box or cabinet, listed by learner name). If a regulator or parent
   ever asks, the paper form is the proof. Make a photo or scan copy and store it somewhere separate.
3. **Enter missing birth years** when you know them ("Learners without a birth year"). It switches on the
   right protections for younger children.
4. **Read safety alerts the same day.** An email titled "safety flag" means the system saw words that may
   show a child is at risk, or hateful words. It is automatic and can be wrong. Talk to the child **in
   person, kindly and casually** (see below), and write down what you did.
5. **Answer privacy requests within 14 days** (7 days if someone only wants a copy of their information).
   They appear on your dashboard as "Privacy requests". If a parent wants a child's account closed, press
   "Lock account now" first, then tell Kevin. Kevin carries out the deletion.
   **Many parents have no email.** A parent may tell you in person (or by phone or WhatsApp). That counts
   as a valid request or withdrawal. Write down the date, the child's name, what was asked and who asked,
   and tell Kevin the same day so the 14 days are not lost. If they want the account closed, press
   "Lock account now" at once.

## When a safety alert arrives

- Do not panic and do not tell the child they were "flagged". Say you noticed they seemed to be working
  hard, and ask how they are.
- If you are worried, ask directly and kindly whether they have thought about hurting themselves. Involve
  their parent or guardian and a local health worker. Do not leave a child who is in danger alone.
- Tell Kevin afterwards (a short message is enough). He keeps the log.
- If the alert is about harm **to** someone else, or abuse, follow your local child-protection duty first.

## Do and do not

- **Do** use only what the platform shows you. **Do not** take screenshots of children's work or chats to
  share on social media or WhatsApp groups without a parent's written permission for that use.
- **Do not** post a child's name, photo, voice or work publicly (social media, flyers, news) unless the
  parent's form clearly says you may. When unsure, use initials.
- **Do not** share logins. Each learner uses their own account.
- **Do** tell Kevin straight away if a phone or laptop with learner data is lost or someone else may have seen
  private information. Speed matters (regulators expect a report within 72 hours).
- **Do** remove access for helpers who leave.
