# Open Mind Africa (Ghana): what to complete before enrolling children

**For Open Mind Africa's leaders. 22 October 2026.** Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) treats every
child's information as special data that needs a parent or guardian's consent. Please complete the blanks below
and return them to Kevin Hallinan. We then finish the Ghana forms and you may begin. **No child is approved on
the platform until a signed parent form is on file.**
